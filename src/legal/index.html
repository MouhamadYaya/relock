Frequently asked questions.

Know what you are choosing.

What is an app blocker, and what does Relock block?

An app blocker puts a barrier between you and an app you keep opening without deciding to. In Relock you pick apps or whole categories in Apple's own picker, then choose how the block behaves: a delay that grows with each opening, a time window such as 10pm–8am, or a daily limit on how many times the app may open.

Does Relock use Apple Screen Time?

Yes. The blocking is performed by Apple's Family Controls framework, the same system behind Screen Time. Relock asks for Screen Time authorization once; without it, it can display your rules but cannot enforce a single one.

Which devices does Relock work on?

iPhone, on iOS 16 or later. Family Controls exists only on Apple platforms, and Relock is built for the iPhone alone — there is no iPad, Mac, Android or web version.

Where is Relock available?

Relock is coming to the App Store in Canada and the United States. It is not published yet: for now you can ask for early access by writing to contact@getrelock.com.

Can Relock see which apps I use?

No. Apple hands Relock opaque selection tokens, not app names, and the implemented blocking flow does not read messages, photos, page contents, or keystrokes from restricted apps. Those tokens stay in the iOS App Group used for enforcement.

Can I schedule app blocking, or limit how often I open an app?

Both. A time-window rule makes the selected apps unavailable during hours you set. A daily open limit caps how many times an app can be opened before it stops opening until tomorrow. You can run several rules at once; the strictest one applying at that moment wins.

Can I unlock an app in a real emergency?

Yes, once a week. The emergency unlock lifts everything immediately — no waiting, no strict mode — and it deletes your rules rather than suspending them. That price is deliberate: it keeps the escape hatch from becoming the habit, and it is what guarantees Relock never holds your phone hostage.

What information is synchronized?

Your account and profile, rule configuration, onboarding answers, and aggregate activity statistics may be stored with Relock service providers. See the Privacy Policy for the exact scope.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Manage or cancel it through the store where you purchased it. Deleting Relock or your Relock account does not itself cancel a store subscription.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. Settings has a JSON export and an account-deletion control you can use yourself, at any time. Data our providers hold for us — your profile photo, your subscription record — is removed within 30 days afterwards. Write to contact@getrelock.com for anything the export leaves out.
