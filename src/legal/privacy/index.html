Privacy Policy

Effective: September 7, 2026 Last updated: September 7, 2026 Version: 1.0

This Policy covers the Relock mobile application and the getrelock.com website. Relock is offered to people aged 16 and over in Canada and the United States.

1. Who we are

Relock is operated by YATECH, a sole proprietorship registered in Quebec, Canada, with its business address at 1187C rue Jogues, Drummondville, Quebec J2B 4X8, Canada ("Relock," "we," "us," or "our"). For applicable privacy laws, YATECH is the controller of — and the enterprise responsible for — the personal data described in this Policy unless stated otherwise. You can reach us at any time at contact@getrelock.com.

2. Scope

This Policy explains how Relock handles personal data when you use the Relock mobile application, visit this website, contact us, or use related services. It does not govern Apple, Google, Supabase, RevenueCat, ImageKit, Sentry, or other third parties acting under their own privacy policies.

4. How we use personal data

We use the data described above to:

  • create and authenticate your account and maintain your session;
  • provide profiles, onboarding, blocking rules, on-device interventions, activity summaries, reminders, and account synchronization;
  • process, validate, restore, and manage subscription access;
  • personalize onboarding and understand subscription conversion using the onboarding attributes sent to RevenueCat;
  • diagnose crashes, app hangs, failed requests, and performance problems when crash reporting is enabled;
  • protect the service, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations; and
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests.

Relock is offered in Canada and the United States. In Canada, and in Quebec in particular, we collect and use personal information with your consent and under the exceptions permitted by the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector and by PIPEDA: information necessary to provide the service you asked for, to bill and support it, to keep it secure, and to meet our legal obligations. Where the law of another jurisdiction applies to you and requires a stated legal basis, we rely on performance of our contract with you, your consent where the product requests it, our legitimate interests in operating and securing Relock where those interests are not overridden by your rights, and compliance with law. We do not use your personal information for automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not profile you for advertising.

6. On-device Screen Time and notification data

Relock uses Apple Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity capabilities. Apple provides opaque tokens for your app/category selections. The implemented design stores those tokens in the iOS App Group shared by Relock and its extensions rather than in Relock’s backend. Local shield-attempt records can include an application key/name and timestamp; Relock converts them into daily aggregate statistics before backend synchronization.

Reminders are scheduled as local device notifications. Relock does not register for remote push notifications, so no reminder content travels through a push server. Notification permission and individual reminder categories can be changed through the app and system settings.

7. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes described in this Policy, or for as long as the law requires. The schedule below is the one we apply.

DataHow long we keep it
Account, profile, rules, onboarding answers, and daily statistics (Supabase)Until you reset the relevant activity or delete your account. Deletion removes the records immediately; residual copies inside our provider’s encrypted backups are overwritten in the normal backup rotation and are gone within 30 days.
Profile photos (ImageKit)Until you replace or remove the photo, or delete your account. Replaced photos and the folder of a deleted account are removed within 30 days by our media clean-up process.
Subscription records (RevenueCat)For as long as your subscription relationship exists, and deleted within 30 days of an account deletion or a deletion request. Purchase and billing records held by Apple or Google are kept by them under their own policies and their own legal retention duties.
Diagnostics (Sentry)Error and performance events are retained for 90 days and then deleted by Sentry. The on-device queue holds at most 60 events awaiting upload.

Website request logs (Cloudflare)Short-lived and held by Cloudflare under its own schedule — currently up to seven days for Workers request logs. We keep no visitor logs of our own.
Data held on your deviceOnboarding progress is cleared when setup completes. Local shield events are removed from the pending log once their aggregate has synchronized. Everything else stored locally is erased when you delete the app.

We may keep information longer where a law, a tax or accounting obligation, a security investigation, or a legal claim requires it. When that reason ends, the information is deleted.

8. Security

Relock uses authenticated Supabase sessions, row-level database policies scoped by user ID, HTTPS service endpoints, server-side privileged keys for account deletion, and short-lived signed ImageKit upload grants. Diagnostic events are configured to exclude default PII and are scrubbed for common secrets such as authorization values, tokens, email addresses, and phone numbers.

Some authentication tokens, preferences, onboarding progress, query cache data, and app state are stored locally on your device using MMKV, protected by the operating system’s own app sandbox and device encryption. We do not add a separate application-level encryption key on top of it, so this Policy does not claim application-level encryption for that local data. No security measure is absolute, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access will never occur.

9. Your in-app controls

  • Profile: edit your display name, birth date, and profile photo.
  • Crash reports: disable or enable crash reporting in Settings. The current default is enabled when Sentry is configured.
  • Notifications: change reminder categories in Relock and notification permission in device settings.
  • Reset activity: delete server-side blocking rules, daily statistics, and block-event rows while keeping the account and subscription relationship.
  • Export: export a JSON file containing your profile, blocking rules, daily statistics, and your most recent 400 block events and 400 daily-statistic rows. For anything the export leaves out — older rows, or data held by a provider on our behalf — write to contact@getrelock.com and we will send it to you.
  • Delete account: delete your account and all of its database records from inside Settings, at any time and without contacting us. Your profile photo and your RevenueCat subscription record are removed within 30 days afterwards. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription — Apple and Google reserve cancellation to you — so cancel it in your store account as well if you no longer want to be billed.

10. Privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent, or objection, and to complain to a privacy regulator. These rights may be subject to legal limits. To make a request, write to contact@getrelock.com. We may need to verify your identity before we act, and we will not charge you for a request or treat you differently for making one.

If you live in Quebec, you may also ask for your information in a structured, commonly used technological format, ask us to stop disseminating information or to de-index a link where the law allows, and complain to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec. If you live elsewhere in Canada, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. If you live in a U.S. state with a privacy law, you may ask to know, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and appeal a refusal by replying to our decision; California residents may also designate an authorized agent.

How we handle a request. One person handles every request: the owner of YATECH, at contact@getrelock.com. We acknowledge your message, verify that it comes from the account holder, and answer within 30 days in Canada and within 45 days under U.S. state laws, extending only where the law allows and telling you why. The in-app export and deletion controls cover everything in our database; for the data our providers hold on our behalf — a profile photo at ImageKit, a subscriber record at RevenueCat, diagnostic events at Sentry — we issue the deletion in each provider’s console and confirm it to you, within 30 days of verifying your request.

11. International transfers

Relock is operated from Quebec, Canada, and every provider we use — Supabase, Sentry, ImageKit, RevenueCat, Cloudflare, Apple, and Google — is established in the United States and runs infrastructure in several countries. Your personal information is therefore stored and may be accessed outside Quebec and outside Canada, primarily in the United States, where laws differ from those of your province, state, or country and where public authorities may in some circumstances obtain access under local law.

Before entrusting personal information to a provider we assess the privacy risk of the transfer, as Quebec law requires, and we rely on written agreements: each provider’s data-processing terms, which bind it to act on our instructions, to apply security measures, to restrict its own sub-processors, and to delete the information at our request — incorporating the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses where that provider’s terms include them. You can ask us which provider holds a given category of information by writing to contact@getrelock.com.

12. Children

Relock is intended for people aged 16 and older. It is not directed to children, it is not marketed to them, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. The birth date in your profile is optional, is used only to personalize the app, and is not an age verification. If you learn that someone under 16 has created an account, write to contact@getrelock.com and we will delete the account and its personal information promptly. Where the law of your jurisdiction sets a higher age for consenting on your own, you may use Relock only with the consent of your parent or guardian.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as Relock changes. We will post the revised version at getrelock.com/privacy and update the "Last updated" date above. When a change materially affects how we handle your personal information — a new category of data, a new purpose, a new recipient, or a longer retention period — we will tell you at least 30 days before it takes effect, through a notice inside the app on next launch and, where we have your address and the law requires it, by email. If the change requires your consent, we will ask for it before the new practice begins, and you may refuse without losing access to the features you already have.
